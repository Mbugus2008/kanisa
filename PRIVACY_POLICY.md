# Privacy Policy for Kanisa App

**Last Updated: December 29, 2025**

## Introduction

Kanisa ("we," "our," or "us") is a church management mobile application developed by Trimline. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Phone Number**: Used as your unique identifier for account registration and login
- **Name**: For member identification within the church community
- **Member Details**: Church-specific information such as baptism status, confirmation status, and group memberships

### Technical Information
- **Device Information**: Basic device identifiers for app functionality
- **Network Data**: Connection status to ensure proper app operation

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Create and manage your church member account
- Display sermons, events, and church announcements
- Enable communication within the church community
- Provide Bible reading features
- Deliver app updates and improvements

## Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored securely on our servers
- We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) for data transmission
- Phone numbers are stored locally on your device for session management
- We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT**:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your information for purposes unrelated to church management

We **MAY** share information:
- With church administrators for member management
- When required by law or legal process

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account
- Opt out of non-essential communications

## Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for general church use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

## App Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions:
- **Internet Access**: Required to communicate with church servers
- **Network State**: To check connectivity before making requests
- **Storage Access**: For downloading and installing app updates

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through the app.
