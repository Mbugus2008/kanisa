# Kanisa App - Household Management Training Guide

## Overview

The Kanisa App allows church members to manage their household/family information. A household consists of:
- **Primary Member** (Head of Household) - The main account holder
- **Spouse** - The primary member's wife/husband
- **Children** - Dependents in the household

---

## 1. Account Types & Roles

| Role | Description | Can Add Children | Can Edit Spouse | Can See Household |
|------|-------------|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Primary** | Head of household | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| **Spouse** | Linked partner | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| **Child** | Dependent | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No |

---

## 2. For Primary Members (Head of Household)

### 2.1 Viewing Your Household

1. Log in to the app with your phone number
2. On the **My Account** screen, you'll see the **"My Household"** section at the top
3. This section displays:
   - Your spouse (if linked) - shown with a pink heart icon
   - Your children (if added) - shown with a blue child icon

### 2.2 Adding Children

**Method 1: From My Account Screen**
1. Go to **My Account**
2. Find the **"My Household"** section
3. Tap the **"Add Child"** button (top right of the section)
4. Fill in the child's details:
   - Name (required)
   - Date of Birth
   - Gender
   - Other optional information
5. Tap **Save** to register the child

**Method 2: From Edit Profile**
1. Go to **My Account** → **Edit Profile**
2. Tap the **child icon** (👶) in the top right corner
3. Fill in the child's details
4. Tap **Save**

### 2.3 Linking to Your Spouse

If your spouse already has a Kanisa account and you want to link your households:

1. Go to **My Account**
2. Tap **"Link to Spouse"** (pink button with family icon)
3. Enter your spouse's registered phone number
4. Tap the **search icon** to find their account
5. If found, their details will appear
6. Tap **"Link Accounts"** to confirm

> ⚠️ **Important**: When you link to your spouse, YOU become a spouse in THEIR household. They become the primary member.

### 2.4 Editing Children's Information

1. Go to **My Account**
2. In the **"My Household"** section, find the child you want to edit
3. Tap the **edit icon** (pencil) on their card
4. Make your changes
5. Tap **Save**

## 3. For Spouses (Linked Partners)

### 3.1 What Spouses Can See

When you log in as a linked spouse, you can:
- ✅ View your own profile
- ✅ See the **"My Household"** section showing:
  - Your spouse (the primary member) - displayed as "Spouse"
  - All children in the household
- ✅ Add new children to the household
- ✅ Edit children's information

### 3.2 What Spouses Cannot Do

- ❌ Edit the primary member's (your spouse's) profile
- ❌ Use the "Link to Spouse" feature (already linked)
- ❌ Unlink from the household (contact church admin)

### 3.3 Adding Children as a Spouse

Spouses can add children the same way as primary members:

1. Go to **My Account**
2. Tap **"Add Child"** in the **"My Household"** section
3. Fill in the child's details
4. Tap **Save**

The child will be added to the household and visible to both you and your spouse.

---

## 4. For Children (Dependents)

### 4.1 What Children Can See

When logged in as a child account:
- ✅ View and edit their own profile
- ✅ Make payments
- ✅ View payment history

### 4.2 What Children Cannot Do

- ❌ See the household section (no "My Household" visible)
- ❌ Add other children
- ❌ Link to any spouse
- ❌ See other family members' accounts

## 6. Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: New Family Registration

1. **Husband registers first** → He becomes the Primary Member
2. **He adds children** → Children are linked to his account
3. **Wife registers separately** with her phone number
4. **Wife uses "Link to Spouse"** → Enters husband's phone → Links to his household
5. **Both can now see** the complete household

### Scenario 2: Wife Registers First

1. **Wife registers first** → She becomes the Primary Member
2. **She adds children**
3. **Husband registers separately**
4. **Husband uses "Link to Spouse"** → Enters wife's phone → He becomes the spouse in her household

### Scenario 3: Adding a New Baby

1. **Either parent** opens **My Account**
2. Taps **"Add Child"**
3. Enters baby's name, date of birth, gender
4. Saves → Baby appears in household for both parents

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why can't I see the "Link to Spouse" button?
**A:** This button is hidden if:
- You're already linked as a spouse
- You're registered as a child
- You already have household members linked to you

### Q: Can I unlink from my spouse?
**A:** Currently, unlinking requires contacting the church administrator who can make changes in the backend system.

### Q: Why can't I edit my spouse's profile?
**A:** For privacy and data integrity, each member can only edit their own profile. Spouses can view each other's information but cannot make changes.

### Q: My spouse doesn't appear in my household. What's wrong?
**A:** Make sure:
1. Your spouse has registered with their own account
2. One of you has used the "Link to Spouse" feature
3. Both accounts are using the correct phone numbers

### Q: Can children make payments?
**A:** Yes, children can make payments and view their payment history from their own accounts.

### Q: Who pays for children - parent or child account?
**A:** Either! Parents can make payments for children, or children can pay from their own accounts. Both options are supported.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Can't find spouse when linking | Verify the phone number is correct and spouse has registered |
| Household section not showing | Check if you're logged in as a child (children don't see this) |
| Add Child button missing | You may be logged in as a child account |
| Changes not appearing | Pull down to refresh, or log out and back in |
| Spouse showing as "Child" | Contact admin - there may be a data issue |
